Xiaolong (Chris) Hou 侯小龙

Assistant Professor of Economics
Lahore University of Management Sciences

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS), Pakistan. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Georgia in 2025.

I specialize in health economics and applied microeconomics. My research focuses on how family relationships and broader social and economic factors shape individual health and health behaviors, with a particular interest in mental health, substance use, and infectious diseases.
